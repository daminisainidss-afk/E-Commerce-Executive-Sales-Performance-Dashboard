# E-Commerce-Executive-Sales-Performance-Dashboard
Interactive E-Commerce Sales &amp; Profitability Dashboard (2018-2020) built with Advanced Excel, Power Query, and Pivot Analysis.

## Executive Summary
This project involves a comprehensive analysis of a multi-channel e-commerce dataset spanning three years. The objective was to transform raw transactional data into a high-level executive dashboard to monitor business health, track regional logistics overheads, and identify high-margin product segments.

## Key Business Impact (Insights Delivered)
By leveraging this dashboard, stakeholders can visualize critical business metrics instantly:

* **Revenue Optimization:** Analyzed a total sales volume of **₹5.38 Crores**.
* **Profitability Analysis:** Identified a net profit of **₹36.85 Lakhs**, with a deep dive into cost vs. revenue.
* **Operational Scale:** Processed and audited **4,806 unique transactions** across global markets.
* **Strategic Growth:** Pinpointed **'Technology'** as the leading profit driver (**₹21.67 Lakhs**), enabling targeted inventory investment.

## Technical Workflow & Features
* **Data Orchestration:** Used **Power Query** to ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) and consolidate disparate datasets from 2018, 2019, and 2020.
* **Advanced Analytics:** Developed complex Pivot models to calculate quarterly growth trends and seasonal spikes.
* **Interactive Visualization:** Implemented dynamic Slicers for 'Region', 'Year', and 'Category' for on-the-fly reporting.
* **Multi-Channel Tracking:** Comparative analysis of sales across major platforms: **Amazon, Flipkart, Snapdeal, and India Mart**.
* **Logistics Auditing:** Monitored shipping costs across **Oceania, Central Asia, and Southeast Asia** to optimize distribution expenses.

## Project Structure
* `Excel_Project_Module_Based.xlsx`: The core interactive dashboard.
* `Data_Source/`: Cleaned CSV/Excel records used for the analysis.
* `Visual_Assets/`: High-resolution snapshots of the dashboard for quick review.
